Unofficial. This page reads the city's public Satellite Court Reservations sheet in your browser and checks it again every few minutes. The sheet is the source of truth.

Open means no reservation is listed for that court. Someone may still be playing on it. Courts are counted from the court numbers that appear in the sheet, so a site may have more courts than shown.

Entries marked ≈ are a best reading of an unclear cell: a time with no AM or PM, or a date that doesn't match its weekday.
